# MediTec Teaching Case – Arbeitsauftrag (September 2026)

Quelle: 2026_MedTech_Case_classroom.pptx (Folien 8–13). Originalsprache Englisch.

## Leitfrage (Folie 8)

What are Michael's options? How should he develop the company in the medium
and long term? Should he think about a partnership, a joint venture, a partial
entry of an investor? Or should he sell it while the brand still has value?
How could he develop his business? What is your recommendation?

## Pitch (Folie 9)

Please record a video in which you pitch your best solution to MediTec's
owner, Michael. Format: voice-over PowerPoint, max. 7 minutes.

Criteria for evaluating the pitch:

1. Stringent storyline/structure (3 points)
2. Novelty and innovative strength of the solution (4 points)
3. Creativity (5 points)
4. Relevance and business impact for Meditec (8 points)

## Assignment 1 (Folie 10, 30 Minuten)

(a) What are Michael's options?
(b) Please develop a recommendation with three time horizons:
- Short-term (0–12 months): What needs to happen immediately?
- Medium-term (1–3 years): How should the business model develop? What skills need to be built?
- Long-term (3–5 years): Where does Meditec stand in 2032?

## Assignment 2: Business Model and Servitization Strategy (Folie 11, 35 Minuten)

(a) Classify Meditec's current offering and the desired transformation on the
product service spectrum. Where does Meditec stand today, where does it want
to go? What could the new business model look like?

(b) What role do digital twins play in the transformation of Meditec? Work out
concretely: What data would a digital twin of a surgical instrument have to
contain? What new business models will be made possible by digital twins? How
do digital twins change the relationship between Meditec and the hospital?
Think of Tim Hagen's problems from the job description: lack of cost
transparency, manual documentation, no real-time visibility.

## Assignment 3: AI Assessment (Folie 12, 45 Minuten)

In the case, Marc describes a three-stage AI framework.

(a) Identify three specific process steps in the sterilization cycle where AI
could add value.
(b) What other AI use cases would be conceivable for Meditec? Identify them,
prioritize them, and describe the impact.
(c) You have a first prototype of the Meditec Customer Agent at your disposal.
Test the agent from the perspective of Tim Hagen (Purchasing). Ask the agent
questions that reflect your day-to-day work. Document: Where does the agent
fail or refuse to respond? What data or system connections are missing? How
should the prototype be further developed?

Rolle: Tim Hagen, Purchasing. You are in charge of medical technology
purchasing at Marien Hospital. They have 14 operating theatres and an
overburdened reprocessing team. They want to know: What's on the agenda this
week? Are there bottlenecks? Are all instruments ready for use?
